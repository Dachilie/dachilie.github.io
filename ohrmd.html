Quick answers

How do I file a leave application?

Download the leave application form from Leave & Attendance, fill it out, and route it to your immediate supervisor and then OHRMD. Read the leave guidelines first for filing periods and attachments.

How do I request my service record or a certificate of employment?

Open Records & Requests, download the request form, and submit it to OHRMD. Processing time is stated on each form’s page.

Where do I find training and seminar opportunities?

See Learning & Development for the training calendar, nomination forms, and on-demand video lessons.

A link or file isn’t opening. What now?

Some files may still be uploading. Contact OHRMD using the details below and mention the title of the resource.
